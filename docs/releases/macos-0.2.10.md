Glassy Desk 0.2.10 improves Fast Connection reliability and protects existing pairings.

- Keeps screen updates fresh on slower connections with bounded video queues and automatic quality adjustment when used with an updated iPhone or iPad app.
- Recovers a fresh picture on an idle desktop without waiting for the screen to change.
- Gives one connected device control of the Mac at a time and reliably releases held keys when control ends.
- Reports screen-sharing and permission problems to compatible clients.
- Preserves your Mac’s pairing identity when Keychain is temporarily unavailable and shows an error instead of silently creating a different identity.

Existing iPhone and iPad versions remain compatible. Your saved Mac identity, update settings, and approved-device credentials are preserved.
