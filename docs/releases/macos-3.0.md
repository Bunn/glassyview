Send files from your iPhone or iPad straight to your Mac's Downloads folder, or get the files selected in Finder. The first time, macOS asks to let Glassy Desk control Finder.
Curtain Mode hides your Mac's screen from anyone nearby while you control it remotely, and ignores the Mac's own keyboard, mouse, and trackpad until you disconnect.
You can turn off file transfers and Curtain Mode in Settings › Security.
Both features need Glassy Desk 3.0 on your iPhone or iPad.
